Privacy Policy - Cliqo Uploader

Last updated: 19 November 2026

What this app is

Cliqo Uploader is a personal desktop tool. It uploads video files from the operator's own computer to the operator's own TikTok account via TikTok's official APIs. It has no other users, no website accounts and no advertising.

Data we receive from TikTok

  • An access token and refresh token issued by TikTok after you authorize the app with TikTok Login Kit.
  • Your TikTok open ID and display name (scope user.info.basic), only to confirm that the correct account is connected.
  • The upload status of videos sent by the app (scope video.upload, or video.publish if enabled).

How the data is stored and used

Tokens are stored only in a local file on the operator's computer. They are used only to upload the operator's own videos and to check their upload status. The app does not have its own server. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising or profiling.

Data we do not collect

The app never asks for or stores your TikTok password. It does not read your followers, messages, likes or other users' data.

Retention and deletion

You can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and services permissions. Deleting the local token file removes all stored data. Tokens also expire automatically.
