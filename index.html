Cliqo Uploader

Cliqo Uploader is a small desktop tool that uploads short gaming clips, edited by the account owner, from the owner's computer to the owner's own TikTok account (@cliqoo.gg) using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Videos are sent to the TikTok inbox as drafts. The account owner reviews each draft in the TikTok app, adds the caption and publishes it themselves.

The tool signs in with TikTok Login Kit. It never asks for or stores a TikTok password.
